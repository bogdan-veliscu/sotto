# Sotto pairing

The K through O driver/navigator rotation was specific to the [soft-launch plan](../../docs/PR_PLAN.md). PR #22 closed the final slice. The original rotation and dispatch prompts are in git history.

For an assigned pairing session, keep one writer per worktree. The driver stays on the assigned wave and hands off the commit, checks run, and open issues. The navigator reviews the diff and cites any blocking defect. The assignment determines who may push or merge.

Follow the standing rules in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md).
