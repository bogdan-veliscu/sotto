Lead-locked. Do not rewrite.

## REQ-SE-001: FTS (INV-FTS-SEARCH)

**EARS:** WHEN a transcript is stored, THE SYSTEM SHALL return it from `search` for a distinctive term present in the golden transcript.

CT-fts-search.

## REQ-SE-002: Delete-all (INV-DELETE-ALL)

**EARS:** WHEN `delete_all` runs, THE SYSTEM SHALL make prior transcript text unsearchable.

CT-delete-all.
