# Design

Quiet editorial desk. High trust. Not a chatbot. Not a SaaS marketing site.

## Signature

A hardware LED next to REC, and an always-visible **on this Mac** chip.

## Type

- Display: Fraunces (variable, a little wonk)
- UI: IBM Plex Sans
- Stamps: IBM Plex Mono

Never Inter, Roboto, Arial, or purple gradients.

## Color

Warm near-black shell `#14110e`, paper `#f3ead8`, ink `#1c1914`, LED `#d23a22`, sage chip text `#b7c4a3`.

Consent cards invert to paper so they feel like a physical disclosure, not a dark-mode modal.
